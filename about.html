About Kloud

Since launching in 2019, Kloud Technologies Limited has operated as an Internet Service Provider and national internet exchange—building dependable connectivity, disciplined operations, and ICT depth that scales from households to enterprises across Bangladesh.

Dedicated support

Launched in 2019, Kloud delivers reliable high-speed internet for homes and businesses, together with corporate connectivity, KloudNIX and BDIX ecosystem access, value-added services, and cloud-aligned solutions—supporting Bangladesh’s digital growth with affordable, resilient access. Our clients rely on stable last-mile and backbone performance, transparent escalation paths, and engineering that treats your uptime as non-negotiable.

We invest in process, monitoring, and people—so capacity upgrades, security posture, and customer care stay aligned as you grow. Whether you need predictable home broadband or a multi-site WAN with managed services, Kloud aims to be the single accountable thread across connectivity and ICT.

Nationwide reach

Coverage across Bangladesh

Kloud’s footprint is built for national scale—from dense urban corridors to regional demand centres—so homes and enterprises can rely on the same engineering standards wherever you connect.

Our operations prioritise predictable last-mile and backbone capacity, coordinated installs, and clear escalation when geography or weather stress the network. The map highlights the breadth of districts and routes we actively support as part of our growth plan.

  • 64 districts in our service scope, aligned with nationwide retail and corporate programmes.
  • Metro, divisional, and emerging growth clusters—one NOC discipline across the map.
  • Corporate WAN, dedicated access, and home broadband—same quality bar, scaled to your location.

Our vision

To be among Bangladesh’s most trusted partners for connectivity and digital infrastructure—where performance, security, and customer experience advance together, without compromise.

Our mission

To empower customers, employees, and partners with resilient internet, expert-managed ICT, and cloud-ready services—backed by clear communication, fair practices, and continuous improvement.
